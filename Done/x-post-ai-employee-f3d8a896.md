# X Post — How My AI Employee Works

## Platform

twitter

## Content

I built an AI Employee that runs 24/7 on my local machine.

Here's what it does automatically:

→ Watches my Gmail & detects new emails
→ Triages each one (priority, type, action needed)
→ Drafts a reply using a rule-based fallback
→ Routes everything to a Pending_Approval folder
→ Nothing is sent until I approve it

It also:
- Polls my Odoo for new invoices & sales orders
- Watches LinkedIn for new messages
- Posts to social media (with approval)
- Generates a weekly CEO Briefing from my business goals
- Audits subscriptions every Friday

The whole thing is built with Python + markdown files as a state machine.

No cloud. No SaaS subscription. Just a vault of folders doing the work.
